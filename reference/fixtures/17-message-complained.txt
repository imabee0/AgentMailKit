- ROOT CAUSE (known, not a harness failure): Gmail does NOT operate a per-message feedback

  ARF/per-message complaint forwarded to the sender's ESP. So a Gmail "Report spam" can never
  produce message.complained regardless of wait time. (Outlook.com/Hotmail JMRP and Yahoo CFL
  DO forward per-message complaints to SES — those are the only viable targets.)
