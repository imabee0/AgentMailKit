stalw.art docs, DkimSignature object (https://stalw.art/docs/ref/object/dkim-signature/):
DKIM signatures are managed objects (WebUI: Management > Domains > DKIM

managed configuration surface. DKIM signing overview:
https://stalw.art/docs/mta/authentication/dkim/sign/
The webadmin edits signatures round-trip through the settings API, i.e. the
running server serves signature.* config entries (incl. private-key) to an
authenticated admin.
