--- (1c) IMPORTANT visibility quirk (anti-abuse) ---
Unauthenticated received messages are EXCLUDED from the list endpoints:
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/messages            -> count=3 (only the "sent" ones)
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/messages?labels=received       -> count=0
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/messages?labels=unauthenticated-> count=0
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/threads             -> count=0
They ARE retrievable individually by id:
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/messages/<url-encoded Message-ID>  -> full object (labels incl. unauthenticated)
  GET /v0/inboxes/amk-probe/threads/<thread_id>                -> thread (message_count etc.)
Consumers relying on the list endpoints will NOT see unauthenticated mail; they must consume the
message.received.unauthenticated webhook (or GET by known id).

================= VERDICT (A14) =================
message.received.unauthenticated is the event for senders that merely fail to authenticate (SPF=none /
no DKIM / no DMARC pass): the message is stored, labeled ["received","unread","unauthenticated"], and the
event fires with full payload (captured verbatim above). A stricter failure (SPF hardfail -all) is handled
differently — accepted at the SMTP gateway but not surfaced (no event, hidden as restricted/spam or dropped).
