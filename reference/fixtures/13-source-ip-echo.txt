## 4. VERDICT

PROVEN. An external client's real public IP (45.233.219.186) reached the whoami pod unaltered
through the externalTrafficPolicy: Local NodePort (31180) on the production single-node k3s at
144.217.66.212. RemoteAddr in the pod = 45.233.219.186:1027. The NodePort was also reachable
from the internet as-is (no exposure.toml entry needed for this port during the probe window).
A5 is CLOSED positive: ETP-Local NodePort preserves source IP; the P6 Multus /32 fallback is
NOT required on source-IP grounds.

Caveats observed:
- Host-originated (hairpin) connections to the NodePort are still SNATed (10.42.0.1) — expected.
- Preservation applies because the single node always hosts the pod; ETP Local would drop traffic
  on a node without a local endpoint (irrelevant here: single-node cluster).
